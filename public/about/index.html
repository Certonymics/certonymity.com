About Certonymity

We are building what the internet should have been: privacy-first, decentralised, and accountable.

Our first product, c.Email, allows users to send and receive end-to-end encrypted emails without relying on third parties or complex setups. Combining advanced cryptography with real-world identity verification, c.Email lets people send private and safe messages from their existing email address – providing both freedom from surveillance and protection from fraud.

Beyond email, Certonymity was founded to solve the problem at the heart of the internet as it is now: how can our need for verified identity and accountability be reconciled with the fundamental right to privacy?

Our answer is cDot: a new network designed to solve the problems of privacy and security everywhere on the internet, from proving who you are to sending messages and moving money.

Collectively, our team has spent decades building successful businesses and software focused on technology and financial services. Alongside the release of the cDot app for secure identity, chat, email, and payments, the cDot framework will soon be available for others to build on — bringing us one step closer to our vision of a secure, permissionless, community-driven internet.
